    Method & interpretation.

    Keyword suggestions match whole words (with short endings, e.g. deadline → deadlines) and are only prompts; you decide every code. Cohen's kappa compares your coding with a CSV exported from this tool by a second coder, per code, matched by response number; values above 0.6 are commonly read as substantial agreement (Landis & Koch 1977).
