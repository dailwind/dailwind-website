A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you begin.

Does analysis require the internet?

No. LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit is designed for fully offline corpus analysis. You will need an internet connection to download the app or open external links.

Is my corpus uploaded to Dailwind Studio?

The offline analysis described here runs on your device. Your corpus is not uploaded to a Dailwind analysis server.

Can I use it in academic research?

Yes. Review the source, check outputs against your research requirements, and cite the version used. Automated tagging and scoring should be interpreted critically.

Where can I report a problem?

Email arslan@dailwind.com or open a GitHub issue. Include your app version, device and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid sending sensitive corpus data.
