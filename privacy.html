Browser corpus toolkit

The separate Corpus Toolkit workspace accepts files and pasted text for analysis entirely in your browser. Corpus content is not sent to a server. Its parsers and algorithms are served as local website assets. By default the workspace exists only for the current page session. The optional Remember setting stores corpus text, metadata, recent queries, saved views and preferences in this browser using IndexedDB and local storage. Turning it off removes saved corpus and preferences while retaining current session text. Clear workspace removes saved toolkit data and current workspace content. Browsers may evict saved data. Export a backup before closing or clearing the workspace. Sharing an export uses your operating system and the destination you choose.

Scope

This policy describes the Dailwind App Studio website, its local research workspaces and the free online PMC workspace. Separately distributed Android apps may have their own policies.

Browsing this website

This website has no advertising or active optional analytics integration. The hosting platform may process technical information such as IP addresses, request logs and similar technical information to deliver, secure and control access to the site. Those platform services operate under their own privacy terms.

Local preferences and cookies

The site saves your privacy choice in your browser’s local storage under dailwind-privacy-v1. It is used only to remember your preference and can be removed by clearing site data. Essential cookies used by the hosting platform are controlled by that platform. The site does not enable optional analytics without an opt-in; no analytics provider is currently connected.

Interactive demonstrations

The phone showcase displays animated screenshots of the app. It accepts no files or pasted text. The separate Corpus Toolkit workspace accepts files for local browser analysis. Preview screens are selected locally; there is no analysis server request or saved preview corpus. Store screenshots are hosted with this website. The embedded preview does not connect to external services.

Contacting us

The email helper creates a draft in your own email application. It does not submit messages to a website database. If you send an email, we receive the information you choose to share, including your email address and message. Email is handled through our Zoho Mail service and your email provider. We use correspondence to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support or ongoing communication.

LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit and your files

LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit is described as a fully offline corpus analysis toolkit. Corpus analysis runs on your device; text is not uploaded to a Dailwind App Studio analysis server. Files and results you choose to share through other applications are handled by those applications. Google Play and Android may process their own download, purchase, diagnostic or platform information under their respective settings and policies.

External services

Links to Google Play, GitHub, Zenodo and ORCID take you to external services. Their cookies, data collection and privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear local site data, change the site’s privacy preferences through Cookie settings, and avoid including sensitive corpus information in correspondence. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have sent directly to us, email arslan@dailwind.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. The updated date will be shown on this page. Contact: Arslan Tahir, Dailwind App Studio — arslan@dailwind.com.

No visitor accounts

The website does not offer visitor accounts. All free tools and the PMC workspace work without signing in. Only the site owner signs in, through Supabase email authentication, to manage PMC content. If you created a member account earlier, email arslan@dailwind.com to have its profile and requests removed.

PMC workspace and paid services

The PMC workspace returns limited excerpts and counts from texts published by the project team. Your search phrases are sent to Supabase to calculate results and are not stored in an account history. Short-lived request counters use a hash of your network address and an hourly global budget to limit usage; these limits are best effort, and counters older than two days are cleared on later searches. Hosting providers may retain their own operational logs. Visible excerpts can be copied or captured; complete source files are not provided. The owner uploads PMC texts to a private Supabase database; only published texts are searchable.

Paid services, including MAT Tagger Cleaner, are arranged by email. Files you send for a paid project reach us through your email provider and are handled as described in Contacting us above.

Reading Room and shared writing

Published articles and PDFs are public website content. Article files and listings are served from this website. Searching the article list happens in your browser and does not submit your search to a server. Contact enquiries use your own email application.
