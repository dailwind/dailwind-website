Browser corpus toolkit

The separate Corpus Toolkit workspace accepts files and pasted text for analysis entirely in your browser. Corpus content is not sent to a server. Its parsers and algorithms are served as local website assets. By default the workspace exists only for the current page session. The optional Remember setting stores corpus text, metadata, recent queries, saved views and preferences in this browser using IndexedDB and local storage. Turning it off removes saved corpus and preferences while retaining current session text. Clear workspace removes saved toolkit data and current workspace content. Browsers may evict saved data. Export a backup before closing or clearing the workspace. Sharing an export uses your operating system and the destination you choose.

Scope

This policy describes this Dailwind Studio website and the offline LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit workflow presented here. Other apps and future releases may have different data practices and should have their own policies.

Browsing this website

This website has no advertising, no public account registration and no active optional analytics integration. The hosting platform may process technical information such as IP addresses, request logs and sign-in information to deliver, secure and control access to the site. Those platform services operate under their own privacy terms.

Local preferences and cookies

The site saves your privacy choice in your browser’s local storage under dailwind-privacy-v1. It is used only to remember your preference and can be removed by clearing site data. Essential cookies used by the hosting or sign-in platform are controlled by that platform. The site does not enable optional analytics without an opt-in; no analytics provider is currently connected.

Interactive demonstrations

The phone showcase displays animated screenshots of the app. It accepts no files or pasted text. The separate Corpus Toolkit workspace accepts files for local browser analysis. Preview screens are selected locally; there is no analysis server request or saved preview corpus. Store screenshots are hosted with this website. The embedded preview does not connect to external services.

Contacting us

The email helper creates a draft in your own email application. It does not submit messages to a website database. If you send an email, we receive the information you choose to share, including your email address and message. Email is handled through our Zoho Mail service and your email provider. We use correspondence to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for support or ongoing communication.

LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit and your files

LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit is described as a fully offline corpus analysis toolkit. Corpus analysis runs on your device; text is not uploaded to a Dailwind Studio analysis server. Files and results you choose to share through other applications are handled by those applications. Google Play and Android may process their own download, purchase, diagnostic or platform information under their respective settings and policies.

External services

Links to Google Play, GitHub, Zenodo and ORCID take you to external services. Their cookies, data collection and privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear local site data, change the site’s privacy preferences through Cookie settings, and avoid including sensitive corpus information in correspondence. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have sent directly to us, email arslan@dailwind.com.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website or its data practices change. The updated date will be shown on this page. Contact: Arslan Tahir, Dailwind Studio — arslan@dailwind.com.
