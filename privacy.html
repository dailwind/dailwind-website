Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

This policy covers LinguaX Arc and all other applications published by Dailwind Studio.

What we collect

Nothing. Our applications do not collect, store, or transmit personal information of any kind.

Your documents and corpora

Files you open in our applications are processed entirely on your device. They are not uploaded to any server, ours or anyone else's. We have no ability to access them. If you uninstall the application, that data is removed with it.

Network activity

Our applications function without an internet connection and do not transmit data during normal operation.

Analytics and advertising

We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking software. There are no third-party SDKs collecting information about you.

Permissions

LinguaX Arc requests access to storage only so that it can open files you select. This access is used for no other purpose.

Children

Our applications are suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data at all, we collect none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
