Browser analysis

Corpus Toolkit is an independent browser beta alongside LinguaX Arc for Android. Its documented statistical tools, lexical sentiment baseline, candidate extraction and experimental context groups require independent review. Grammatical profiles rely on imported dependency annotations; automatic multilingual tagging is not included. Validate outputs before academic or professional use. Browser memory and file format limitations apply. Keep backups; we do not retain a server copy of your corpus. The Android archive citation refers to its archived release, not to a separate peer-reviewed validation of the browser edition.
