How to interpret the numbers

Words are Unicode letter/number sequences with internal hyphens and apostrophes. Unique words can be case sensitive. Sentences are punctuation-based estimates; paragraphs use blank lines. These counts can differ from Microsoft Word or another tokenizer. CoNLL and CSV files are counted as raw text, not parsed annotation or data tables. No grammatical annotation, language identification or automatic genre classification is claimed. In languages without word spacing, these are token counts, not linguistic word segmentation.

Source title, author, year, publication, URL, DOI, ISBN, language and rights are user-supplied context. Filename and first-line titles are suggestions. File modification dates describe the local file, not its publication date. Subtitle duration is the first-to-last cue span, including gaps.
