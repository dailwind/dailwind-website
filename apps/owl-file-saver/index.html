Owl File Saver

Files & storage · Android

Live on Google Play

A private, offline file saver for Android. Save shared files in one tap, organise them and keep them on your device.

Owl File Saver is a private, offline file saver, storage organiser and status vault for Android. Save, organise and safeguard shared files, photos, videos and statuses directly on your device, with no internet access required.

Features

  • One-tap save from the Android share sheet to the storage folder you choose.
  • Status saver for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business, with a fullscreen viewer.
  • Built-in video player with touch controls and a mute toggle.
  • Day and dark themes, including an OLED-friendly dark mode.
  • App lock with a private offline 4-digit PIN.
  • History with filters (images, documents, media, archives) and search by filename or extension.
  • No internet permission, no analytics and no ads. Your files never leave your device.
