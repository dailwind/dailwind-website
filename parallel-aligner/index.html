Sentence alignment

Method & interpretation.

Alignment uses the length-based method of Gale & Church (1993): sentence lengths in letters and digits are compared against the overall length ratio of the two texts, allowing 1–1, 1–2, 2–1, 2–2 and unmatched sentences. No dictionary is used, so check rows where whole sentences were added or dropped.
