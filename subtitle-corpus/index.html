Subtitle Grabber extension

Checking…

YouTube does not let websites read subtitles directly. The small Dailwind extension collects them from your own browser, the same way the YouTube player does, and gives them only to this page. Nothing is sent to Dailwind.

  1. Download the extension (ZIP) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped subtitle-grabber folder.
  5. Come back here and refresh this page.

Works on a computer. On a phone, or for a very large job, use the paid service below.

Good to know

  • Research use. Subtitles belong to their owners. Use the corpus for study and research, cite the sources (the metadata CSV lists every video) and do not republish full subtitle texts.
  • Auto-generated and machine-translated text contains recognition and translation errors. The metadata sheet records which kind each file is, so you can report it in your method.
  • Pace. The page asks YouTube for one video at a time with a short pause. A 100-video playlist takes a few minutes. If YouTube slows you down, wait and press Build again; finished videos are kept.
  • Private. Links and subtitles stay in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded to Dailwind.
