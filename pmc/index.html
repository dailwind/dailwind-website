THE PROJECT

Medical discourse.
A shared research resource.

The Pakistan Medical Corpus (PMC) is a joint venture of Dr. Adnan Tahir and Dr. Muhammad Afzaal dedicated to analyzing medical discourse in Pakistan.

The project team describes a collection spanning hundreds of millions of words/tokens, covering medical journals, blogs, case studies and e-health communication. This project-reported scale is not the size of the demonstration below; a verified inventory and counting method will accompany the live release.

PMC supports research into grammatical complexity, lexical variation, metadiscourse and medical-education terminology. Academic collaborations, corpus processing and a multidisciplinary team support its development toward an accessible research platform.

Future aims include research on medical communication, AI-driven healthcare applications and policy, with institutional partnerships and subscription models supporting sustainable access.
