Understand this analysis

Results are calculated from the published collection.

Word tokens use Unicode letters/numbers with internal apostrophes or hyphens. Matching is case-insensitive. Word forms are not lemmas; grammatical tagging and syntactic word sketches are not included. The type-token ratio depends strongly on corpus length.

About the collection & research access

The project team reports a collection of hundreds of millions of words/tokens across medical journals, blogs, case studies and e-health communication. A verified inventory and counting method will accompany the release. The live preview contains only the texts published by the project team, not the full corpus.

Research areas include grammatical complexity, lexical variation, metadiscourse and medical-education terminology. Future development includes institutional partnerships and sustainable subscription access.

Public preview searches return limited excerpts rather than complete documents. Visible excerpts can still be copied or captured. Full-collection access is arranged directly with Dailwind; there is no public corpus download.

For linguistic research. This workspace does not provide medical advice.
