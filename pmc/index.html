About the collection & research access

The project team reports a collection of hundreds of millions of words/tokens across medical journals, blogs, case studies and e-health communication. A verified inventory and counting method will accompany the release. The public sample above is separate from that collection.

Research areas include grammatical complexity, lexical variation, metadiscourse and medical-education terminology. Future development includes institutional partnerships and sustainable subscription access.

Approved preview searches return limited excerpts rather than complete documents. Visible excerpts can still be copied or captured. Full-collection access is arranged directly with Dailwind; there is no public corpus download.

For linguistic research. This workspace does not provide medical advice.
