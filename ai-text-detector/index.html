Signals behind the estimate

Signals: burstiness (variation in sentence length), stock phrases common in generated prose, MTLD lexical diversity, repeated three-word sequences, contractions and personal voice, and structural uniformity. They are combined with fixed weights; there is no language model and nothing is sent anywhere. Short texts, lists, poetry, code and translated text give unreliable results.
