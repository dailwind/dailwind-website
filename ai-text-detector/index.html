Signals behind the estimate

Signals include burstiness, stock AI-style phrases, formulaic transitions, empty intensifiers, MTLD, repeated trigrams, voice (re-weighted for academic register), structural uniformity, mid-length sentence share, paragraph variation and function-word rhythm. Weights are fixed and visible; there is no language model and nothing is sent anywhere. Short texts, lists, poetry, code and heavy translation give unreliable results.
