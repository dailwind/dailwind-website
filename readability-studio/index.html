Method & interpretation.

Readability. Flesch Reading Ease (higher = easier), Flesch–Kincaid grade, Gunning Fog, SMOG (McLaughlin 1969), Coleman–Liau and ARI; the consensus grade is the mean of the five grade formulas. Formulas only see sentence and word length, not meaning or reader knowledge.

MTLD (McCarthy & Jarvis 2010) is the mean length of word runs that keep a type–token ratio above 0.72, averaged forwards and backwards. HD-D is the expected proportion of types in a random 42-token sample (hypergeometric). Both are far less sensitive to text length than raw TTR.
