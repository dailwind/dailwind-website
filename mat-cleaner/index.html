What we prepare.

We clean and organise plain-text corpora so they work smoothly in MAT: encoding problems, invisible and control characters, OCR artefacts, broken line-end hyphens and spacing are fixed; very short or empty files are flagged; and every file is accounted for in a clear preparation report.

Already tagged your texts? We can also check MAT output before counting, including files that cause the zero-baseline error.

The full job, if you need it.

Want the whole workflow handled? Ask in the same email about MAT tagging and multidimensional analysis of your corpus, including large projects of millions of words.

How it works.

1. Email us your task: the number of files and words, the language, and what you need back.
2. We agree the scope, timing and price with you before any work begins.
3. You receive the prepared corpus with a report of every change.
