Data and performance

All corpus processing stays in your browser. Analyses run in a dedicated worker and time out after 30 seconds (60 seconds for corpus indexing) rather than blocking the page indefinitely. Limit: 50 files per corpus; 20 million characters and 1 million tokens across target + reference; 10 million characters and 20 MB per file; 1,000 pages per PDF; 10,000 aligned rows.

Corpus backups contain your text. Sharing or downloading a file is your own choice. Browser storage may be cleared by the device; keep an exported backup. PDF and DOCX readers load from this site. Scanned PDFs need OCR before import. No offline PWA is enabled.
