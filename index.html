Why offline

Most research software assumes a laptop, a stable connection, and permission to upload your data to somebody else's server. For a large part of the world, all three assumptions fail at once.

A student in Multan may own a phone and nothing else. A fieldworker recording interviews may have no signal for days. A researcher working with legal or clinical transcripts may be contractually forbidden from uploading them anywhere.

Building offline-first solves all three at once. It is also simply better software: faster, private by construction, and it keeps working when everything else stops.
