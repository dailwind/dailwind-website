Method & interpretation.

Burrows' Delta (Burrows 2002) converts relative frequencies of the most frequent words into z-scores across your texts and averages the absolute differences. Cosine Delta (Smith & Aldridge 2011; Evert et al. 2017) uses the angle between z-score vectors and is often more robust. Eder's Delta weights higher-ranked words more.

Delta shows relative stylistic similarity within the texts you supply. It is evidence for exploration, not a verdict on authorship; genre, period and length all influence results.
