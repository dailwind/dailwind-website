02 / CHOOSE THE DIVISION

Your pages. Your parts.

One part per range. Ranges must be ordered, non-overlapping and within your selected pages. Unlisted pages are omitted.

Number-of-parts mode balances page counts; file sizes can differ because pages contain different amounts of data. For example, 1,000 pages divided into 20 parts gives 50 pages per part.

Your division preview will appear after opening a PDF.

02 / MAKE SHARING EASIER

A smaller copy, with a clear tradeoff.

Compression processes the complete PDF. First try structural optimization: if it produces a larger file, the original-size copy is offered instead, with no false reduction claim. For image-heavy scanned books, choose Smaller scan and adjust resolution/quality. This rasterizes pages and can reduce legibility if set too low.

No exact target size or compression percentage is guaranteed. Review the resulting PDF before sharing it.
