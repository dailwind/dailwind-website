> I told you in the start
> Wait not and trust not
> Don’t go beyond your limited jurisdictions
> Because the love creates more than quanta frictions
> Stanza 1

> They shaded a lot of tears
> Whom they separated closest and nears
> Lovers lost famous dynasties and right ways
> Never back they went such ways
> Stanza 2

> Wishes in clutches and distance their fate
> Sprout agonies and comforts late
> Some dealt friendly, some rudely
> Nobody knows why they dealt proudly
> Stanza 3

> So be careful and not let anyone
> Not give sufferings to fortune
> Wait not and show not
> Trust not and hate not
> Stanza 4

# About the poet

Muhammad Aqeel is a poet and a PhD scholar in the Department of English at the University of Sialkot. His research interests include English literature and linguistics.
