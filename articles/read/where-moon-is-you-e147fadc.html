Poetry

Where Moon is You

Arslan · 2026-11-03 · Online reading

An English love poem about a land of lights where the beloved is the moon.

Don't make me know
What I knew.
I'm just a star where Moon is you.
Hold my hand,
Let me show you.
The land of lights where moon is you.
Sun there shines like eyes of you.
Heaven there calls the names of you.
Castles there made with jewels like you.
Water there fall like hairs of you.
Flowers there scent smell of you.
Birds there sing songs of you.
Songs of you.
How pretty is my world made of you.
Where brightness of your grin greens my dirt.
Where stars in your eyes bright my nights.
Hold my hand,
Let me show you.
The land of lights
where moon is you.
Where Moon is You...

~Arslan~
